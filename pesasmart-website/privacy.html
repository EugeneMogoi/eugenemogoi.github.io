Privacy policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Applies to the PesaSmart app for Android.

In short

  • PesaSmart reads money messages (and, if you turn them on, bank notifications and bank emails) to build your financial records.
  • Everything is processed and stored on your phone. We do not run a server that receives your transactions.
  • We never sell your data, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models.
  • You can delete all your data at any time.

1. Who we are

PesaSmart is provided by Eugene Mogoi, Nairobi, Kenya ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the purposes of the Data Protection Act, 2019. Contact: eugenemogoi@gmail.com.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To build your records: money in and out, categories, balances, loans, events, budgets, investments and reports.
  • To detect duplicates, transfers between your own accounts, loan repayments and missing messages.
  • To show you alerts and summaries inside the app.

We process this data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you grant each permission and turn on each optional feature. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the feature off or removing the permission in your phone's settings.

4. Where your data is kept

Your data is stored in PesaSmart's private storage on your phone and is processed there. It is not uploaded to us. If you create a backup, it is saved as a file to a place you choose, such as your phone or your own cloud drive, and is controlled by you.

5. Background processing

To keep your figures up to date, PesaSmart works in the background on your phone. It:

  • reads new money messages when they arrive;
  • catches up on messages it missed while the phone was off or the app was closed;
  • pairs transfers between your own accounts and matches loan repayments;
  • checks balances for missing messages and recalculates totals;
  • if you connected Gmail, checks for new bank emails when the phone is online;
  • runs the same checks when you pull down to refresh.

You can limit background activity in your phone's battery settings. Some figures may then update only when you open the app.

6. Google user data (Gmail)

If you connect Gmail, PesaSmart requests read-only access to find emails from bank and payment senders. These emails are processed on your phone to extract transaction details. The email content is not stored after processing, is not sent to us or anyone else, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. No person reads your emails.

PesaSmart's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Gmail in PesaSmart's settings or in your Google Account's security settings at any time.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. Data leaves your phone only when you choose: for example when you export a report or save a backup. Google provides the sign-in for Gmail; Google's own privacy policy applies to that.

8. How long we keep it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Settings › Delete all data removes it, and so does uninstalling the app. Backup files you saved are kept wherever you put them until you delete them.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, to access it, to object to its processing, to have it corrected and to have it deleted. Because your data is on your phone, you can view, correct, export and delete it yourself in the app. For any request or question, contact us at eugenemogoi@gmail.com.

10. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add a PIN lock in Settings. Keep your phone locked and your backups in a safe place.

11. Children

PesaSmart is not intended for anyone under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.

13. Contact and complaints

Email eugenemogoi@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya.
