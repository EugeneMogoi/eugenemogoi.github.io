How it works

No typing in receipts. No bank logins. PesaSmart starts from the messages you already get.

It reads your money messages

M-PESA, banks, saccos, lenders and Ziidi. It uses only money messages; other messages are skipped.

It sorts them for you

Each payment gets a category, its fee is recorded separately, and duplicates are removed.

It asks when it isn't sure

Money from a person or a bank could be income, a loan, a gift or your own money. PesaSmart puts it in “Please confirm” instead of guessing.

It shows the true picture

Real income and spending, with loans and transfers between your own accounts kept out, so the numbers mean what they say.

What PesaSmart does in the background

So you open the app to figures that are already up to date. All of this happens on your phone.

Reads new messages as they arrive

A new M-PESA or bank message is read and filed within seconds, with or without internet.

Catches up on missed messages

If the phone was off or the app was closed, it goes through the inbox and picks up anything it missed, without creating duplicates.

Pairs and matches

It links transfers between your own accounts, matches repayments to the loans they pay off, and joins a bank message with the matching M-PESA message.

Checks for gaps

It compares the balance in each message with the one before. If money moved without a message, it tells you a message may be missing.

Keeps totals current

Balances, budgets, loan balances, events and investments are recalculated with one set of rules, so every screen and report agrees.

Bank emails, when you turn it on Coming

With Gmail connected, it checks for new bank emails when you're online, read-only and from bank senders only. Bank app alerts can be read from your notifications.

Pull down to refresh on any main screen to run these checks straight away. PesaSmart never sends messages, never moves money and never contacts your bank.

Everything in one place

Categories, splits and notes

Split one payment into parts, add a short note, and teach PesaSmart once: it remembers how to file the same person next time.

Loans

Money you borrowed from people and lenders, with repayments matched and a running balance. Fuliza, M-Shwari and lender loans included. Tracking money you lent is coming.

Events

Tag a birthday, wedding or trip and see what it really cost: spent, gifts received and net cost, with borrowed money shown separately.

Budgets and bills

Spending limits, bills and savings goals for the month, with what's left to spend on the dashboard.

Investments

Ziidi savings and shares, with profit counted at average cost, so selling shares isn't mistaken for income.

Reports and exports

Monthly statements and reports by category, person or account, exported as PDF, Excel or CSV.

Your look

Six themes, each in light and dark, or matching your phone.

Backup you control Coming

Save one backup file wherever you choose and restore it on a new phone.

Private by design

  • Stays on your phone. PesaSmart has no server holding your transactions. No account to create.
  • Money messages only. PesaSmart uses messages from M-PESA, banks and other money services. Other messages are skipped and never stored.
  • No ads, no selling. Your data is never sold, shared for advertising or used to train AI.
  • Delete anytime. Settings › Delete all data, or uninstall, removes everything.

Questions

Does PesaSmart move money or need my PIN?

No. It only reads messages. It cannot send money, pay bills or see your PIN, and it never asks for bank passwords.

Do I need internet?

No. Reading SMS, sorting and every screen work offline. Internet is only used for optional features such as Gmail bank emails and saving a backup to a cloud drive you choose.

Which banks and services does it understand?

M-PESA (including Fuliza and M-Shwari), Ziidi, and messages from banks, saccos and lenders such as KCB, Equity, Stanbic, Family Bank, Choice, Stima Sacco and others. New formats are added regularly.

What if it files something wrongly?

Tap the payment and change it. You can ask PesaSmart to remember your choice for that person or business.

Why does it ask for SMS permission?

Reading your money messages is how PesaSmart builds your records. Without it, you can still import statements by hand.
